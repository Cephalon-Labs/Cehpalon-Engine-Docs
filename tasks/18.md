## Configurable authentication and tenant read sets - 2026-11-20

User-authorized refinement adds frozen algorithm/key/metadata profiles and established ticket mapping. Same-login tenant selection validates the destination afresh; no singleton current tenant or wildcard/global-admin bypass. Aggregate reads require a separate trusted principal/operation/resource-bound grant of at most 64 tenants and repeat normal per-tenant authorization. Null selection means every tenant in that grant; explicit subsets cannot expand it. No partial response is returned after denial/fault/cancel; earlier authorized reads may have executed. Parent-linked required audit preserves control-plane versus tenant-history boundaries.
