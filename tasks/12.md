## PostgreSQL restricted runtime pool proof

A disposable PostgreSQL 18.6 Linux fixture with Npgsql 10.0.3 and .NET 11 GA verified same-backend reuse under a restricted runtime login. Both commit and rollback cleared transaction-local tenant scope. The unscoped read saw zero rows and tenants A and B each saw their own row for the shared order ID. Default SQL rollback, explicit false and refusal before opening a pool with a missing password were verified separately. The owned fixtures and loopback listeners were removed. This covers the PostgreSQL fixture boundary. Application provider, migrations and full Business workload gates remain open.

[Validation and scoped results](https://github.com/Cephalon-Labs/Cehpalon-Engine-Docs/blob/main/internal/validation/postgresql-runtime-pool.md) · [Samples proof](https://github.com/Cephalon-Labs/Cehpalon-Engine-Samples/pull/2)


## Application-facing PostgreSQL order proof

The optional Samples consumer used Engine-checked tenant scopes with PostgreSQL 18.6 on WSL. An explicit separate-role migration passed atomic rollback, initial apply and repeat refusal. Six invalid or denied scope cases performed zero store checkout and SQL. Two tenants read distinct values at the same order ID. The eligible stale revision changed zero rows, the authorized transition committed, and cancellation after an actual update preserved the original row. Same-backend scope clearing and owned container, volume and loopback cleanup were verified. The remaining provider, identity and Business workload gates are open.

[Evidence and scope](https://github.com/Cephalon-Labs/Cehpalon-Engine-Docs/blob/main/internal/validation/postgresql-application-scope.md)


## Concurrent PostgreSQL conditional order operations

The restricted application runtime on PostgreSQL 18.6 and Npgsql 10.0.3 verified overlapping READ COMMITTED writes by distinct tenants on distinct backends, one committed transition and one zero-row conditional result with a server-observed same-row blocker, and cancellation during a confirmed lock wait. The canceled waiter acknowledged rollback and its exact backend returned without tenant scope or visible rows. Existing migration, scope and eligible stale-revision controls passed. This scope leaves wider provider, identity and Business workload gates open.

[Protocol and validation](https://github.com/Cephalon-Labs/Cehpalon-Engine-Docs/blob/main/internal/validation/transactions-and-drain.md)
