# Observability contracts and sensitive-data requirements

Status: **Draft for review — M0 (Planned / Experimental)**. Date: 2026-11-26.
Scope: first acceptance item of [Engine #18](https://github.com/Cephalon-Labs/Cehpalon-Engine/issues/18) only ([REQ-10 coverage](coverage.md)). See the [task mirror](../../tasks/18.md), [program plan](../planning/2026-10-27-program-plan.md) and [delivery and maturity standard](../standards/delivery-and-maturity.md). Names, limits and rules below are proposed unless identified as existing behavior. This draft neither authorizes a runtime change nor qualifies a deployment profile.

## Evidence and compatibility boundaries

- [Dispatch correlation](../validation/dispatch-correlation.md) shows a selected opt-in Core completion callback at Engine `5e92ac1c7fa1e150e7e97de6d68e94b31ffba053`. It captures optional detached ambient W3C IDs at entry and reports the outcome and duration after handler cleanup. The protected HTTP and GraphQL fixture reused ASP.NET's native request Activity and a host-owned in-memory sink on Windows and WSL. One outer gateway record is not one HTTP request or one histogram point: nested dispatches also contribute to the histogram, anonymous rejection can precede dispatch, and sibling GraphQL fields can share the request span IDs. Missing and malformed contexts were compared with native host behavior; a new ingress trust policy, child spans and cross-process propagation were not demonstrated. Preserve [callback semantics](../planning/observability-trace-correlation.md): isolate exceptions, suppress same-flow recursion and exclude the callback from the measured interval.
- [Dispatch and lifecycle metrics](../validation/dispatch-lifecycle-metrics.md) establish bounded local histograms, best-effort publication and failure isolation at Engine `c18757f7ef4df2c78c73e902aa39d05424e6bcff`. [Budget gauges](../planning/budget-metrics.md) are separate, host-owned aggregate observations, not tenant identifiers or one atomic snapshot. These results do not establish exporter behavior.
- [Business replay evidence](../validation/business-replay-and-ga-build.md) covers a scoped PostgreSQL/RabbitMQ recovery fixture, not telemetry propagation. [ADR-0018](../adr/ADR-0018-tooling-trace-guided-investigation.md) and [ADR-0019](../adr/ADR-0019-tooling-same-process-wait-traces.md) govern separate, perturbed #20 tooling investigations. EventPipe, native traces, PID/TID and private diagnostic artifacts are not inputs to this contract or proof that it works.

## Signal contract (draft v1)

The host owns telemetry clocks, configuration, sampling, sinks and their lifecycle. Adapters map protocols; portable handlers do not receive transport, collector or vendor types. New signal names are fixed lowercase `cehpalon.*` names. Incompatible changes to names, fields, units or meanings require a reviewed contract revision. Preserve native instruments and their vocabulary. A disabled telemetry module starts no telemetry-owned exporter, connection, timer or background work; independent framework diagnostics are subject to the same policy before export.

### Traces

These are proposed logical names for exported spans. For an instrumented host boundary, project the native span rather than create a duplicate server span. If a safe projection is unavailable, do not export that span under this contract. The existing Core callback creates no span and does not imply span export.

| Span name | Kind | Required `transport` | Timed boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cehpalon.request` | `SERVER` | `http`, `graphql`, `grpc`, `jsonrpc`, or `sse` | Adapter ingress through owned call or stream completion, including its cleanup; not time to first byte or proof of client receipt. |
| `cehpalon.dispatch` | `INTERNAL` | Same finite set, plus `amqp` and `local` | Registered Core invocation, from entry through owned handler cleanup; not the full wire request. This proposed child span is distinct from the callback. |
| `cehpalon.message.publish` | `PRODUCER` | `amqp` | One publish attempt, including confirmation where the profile waits for it; never a consumer acknowledgement or commit. |
| `cehpalon.message.process` | `CONSUMER` | `amqp` | One Worker delivery attempt through owned work and disposition; not a deduplicated count of business effects. |

A completed span requires `name`, `kind`, valid W3C `trace_id` and `span_id`, `start_utc`, `end_utc`, monotonic elapsed `duration_ms`, `transport` and `outcome`. Attach `parent_span_id` only for a real parent; a link is a separate typed pair of trace/span IDs. Allow at most one link: for multiple causal inputs, start an unlinked root rather than pick an arbitrary parent. No arbitrary span events, free-form status text or extra attributes. Duration must be finite and nonnegative. Omit a span whose required fields cannot be produced; never fabricate context or timing.

New exported spans and logs use one of five `outcome` values: `success` (normal completion), `failure` (expected rejection or business failure), `cancelled` (classified cancellation), `fault` (unexpected execution or owned cleanup fault), or `unknown` (no reliable classification). Apply it at the specific boundary; a successful HTTP envelope can carry failed GraphQL fields. Project native outcomes without changing their meaning, caller-visible exceptions or native metric tags. A disconnect alone does not establish operation cancellation; no signal proves rollback or durability.

### Structured logs

| Fixed event name | Required fields | Optional fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cehpalon.operation.dispatch.completed` | `event_name`, `timestamp_utc` (when the host observes completion), `severity`, `outcome`, `duration_ms` | `trace_id` and `span_id` together, omitted if absent, invalid, non-W3C or not permitted by the boundary policy |

This is a proposed host-side envelope, not a claim about a shipped logger or an existing literal. Project one record per notification from an explicitly selected dispatcher. Use structured fields only: `severity` is `info` for `success`, `failure` or `cancelled`; `error` for `fault`; `warn` for `unknown`. No free-form message. The two IDs refer to the same Activity captured at dispatch entry, not a unique completion key or proof the span was sampled/exported. Skip invalid records rather than fall back to raw data. Correlation fields may be used for restricted diagnostic lookups, never as dimensions, grouping labels or exemplars.

### Metrics

| Existing instrument | Type and unit of observation | Required measurement attributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Cehpalon.Engine.Core` / `cehpalon.operation.dispatch.duration` | Histogram in seconds (`s`); one recorded Core invocation, including nested invocations where instrumented | Native `outcome` only |
| `Cehpalon.Engine.Composition` / `cehpalon.module.lifecycle.duration` | Histogram in seconds (`s`); one recorded lifecycle attempt per resource | Native `phase` (`activation`, `rollback`, `disposal`) and `outcome` only |
| `cehpalon.operation_budget.active`, `cehpalon.operation_budget.capacity`, `cehpalon.operation_budget.closed`, `cehpalon.operation_budget.active_tenants`, `cehpalon.operation_budget.tenant_capacity` | Integer observable gauges in `{operation}`, `{operation}`, unitless (0/1), `{tenant}`, and `{operation}` respectively; last two are tenant-budget only | None |

Treat histogram counts as observations, not wire request totals, and missing measurements as missing, not zero. Budget meters may use stable names from trusted deployment config, never interpolated identities. Confirm native outcome values at the pinned revision before enabling export; reject an incompatible mapping rather than rename the native values or add dimensions.

**Proposed cardinality ceiling:** two histogram names and five gauge names. Allow at most eight distinct native `outcome` values per histogram and three phases, giving at most 8 Core and 24 Composition measurement label sets. Allow at most two budget meter names, with one empty label set per gauge per meter (at most 10 gauge series). Total ceiling: 42 logical series per fixed service/environment, excluding histogram bucket expansion and backend-generated series. Only `service.name` and `deployment.environment.name` may group resource series, with values fixed by a deployment allowlist; no instance, pod, host, customer or tenant labels. Reject out-of-contract points without creating series. No metric exemplars. Trace/log event names and enum values are finite by schema; only explicit correlation fields may hold high-cardinality IDs.

## Context propagation and trust boundaries

Use [W3C Trace Context Level 1](https://www.w3.org/TR/2021/REC-trace-context-1-20211123/) for new bridges. V1 accepts exactly one `traceparent` version `00` (55 ASCII bytes, lowercase hex, nonzero trace and parent IDs, valid flags syntax). Duplicate, combined, malformed, oversize and unsupported versions are absent for telemetry purposes. `tracestate`, `baggage` and custom correlation fields must not influence context or be forwarded. Only a configured, authenticated internal peer in the same approved trust domain can supply a parent. Otherwise create a new local root if enabled, without a link, and do not export or echo the caller-supplied IDs. End-user identity or tenant selection does not make a caller a trusted peer. Remote sampling flags cannot override local policy. Enforce these rules before remote input determines exported identity, including IDs captured by completion logs. If the host cannot do so, suppress affected span export and omit affected log IDs until qualified. No change to acceptance, authorization, deadlines, retries or business results; missing context is not an application error.

| Transport or host | Draft rule | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASP.NET HTTPS HTTP and GraphQL, Small API on Windows/WSL | Let the host own request Activity and parsing. Apply policy before its Activity can be used for contract correlation. Keep context request-local and flow it into owned async dispatch/parallel fields. Siblings must not mutate the request context; aliases, query names and arguments are not keys. End the request span at the host boundary, not at first field completion. | Native Activity reuse and selected callbacks observed. The peer policy and new spans are unproven. See [protected adapters](../adr/ADR-0008-protected-http-graphql-adapters.md). |
| Same-process Core/Composition, console or unified host | Flow approved context within owned async work and restore prior context on exit, including cancellation and fault. Across a suppressed execution context, pass a detached snapshot only for an explicit owned handoff. Without a current Activity the callback leaves IDs absent; separately enabled boundary tracing may start a root. Never keep an Activity, principal or transport object in a singleton, pool or unrelated job. | Detached Core capture observed; no new in-process span or queue contract proven. |
| Business outbox, RabbitMQ and Worker | [ADR-0003](../adr/ADR-0003-multitenant-business-profile.md) reserves trace context in the Business envelope. Carry at most one validated `traceparent` in immutable event metadata, bound to the trusted producer and persisted with the outbox. Relay retries reuse it, never overwrite it with ambient relay context. A publish or delivery attempt gets a new root span and at most one link to the verified original context, or no link if invalid/absent. Do not echo context in ACKs, poison diagnostics or public replay records. The Worker separately establishes service identity and checks tenant scope. | Recovery is evidenced only for the selected fixture; encoding, trusted provenance and replay propagation are not proven. |
| gRPC, JSON-RPC and SSE | gRPC: initial request metadata, one scope per call/stream, not per message/trailer. JSON-RPC over HTTP: request headers, never params or RPC IDs; no context leakage across batch items. No non-HTTP mapping proposed. SSE: initial HTTP carrier through stream close/cancel; reconnect is a new request, with no context derived from `Last-Event-ID` or event data. | Planned adapters in [#10](../../tasks/10.md), not propagation evidence. |
| Outbound HTTP/gRPC from an application, Worker or AI/Retrieval adapter | Inject at most one newly serialized `traceparent` from the current approved local context, only to an allowlisted internal destination. Never forward a caller's header verbatim, send it to external providers or put it into prompts, documents or other business payloads. The only proposed persistence exception is the declared event metadata. | [AI/Retrieval lane](../adr/ADR-0004-ai-retrieval-profile.md) and cross-host rules are not evidence of implementation. |
| Native server, container, proxy, Kubernetes, serverless and edge | Apply policy at every ingress/egress, never infer trust from placement or forwarded headers. Proxies pass only the approved carrier on trusted internal hops. Reset scope per independently scheduled Worker job or warm invocation. Export scope stays within the host-owned lifetime; no assumed background flush after response or eviction. | [Deployment profiles](../../tasks/21.md); Windows/WSL fixtures and hosted contract checks do not establish these deployment profiles. |

## Sensitive-data policy

1. **Allowlist before capture.** Construct only the approved fields before any telemetry callback, queue, sampler, sink, exporter or fallback logger. Apply the same rules to automatic diagnostics or exclude those sources. Collectors enforce the allowlist again before storage or forwarding; collector redaction is not a source control. Encode structurally, reject invalid values, and drop records with invalid required fields. Any separately reviewed loss accounting uses aggregate counts or fixed reason codes, never rejected input.
2. **Never emit** credentials, passwords, tokens, cookies, session IDs, keys, certificates or connection strings; raw headers/carriers, baggage, request/response bodies, untemplated URLs/paths/query strings, GraphQL documents/variables, RPC parameters, SSE data, broker contents, SQL statements/values, exception messages/stacks, config dumps, personal/customer data (including email and client IP), prompts/model outputs/tool I/O or retrieved content. Exclude raw claims, resource IDs, broker topology IDs, operation/module identifiers and caller-supplied correlation values. Only the explicitly permitted detached W3C IDs may correlate telemetry; the carrier itself is not a signal field.
3. **Omit rather than partially mask.** Drop a prohibited field, not just substrings. Do not retain prefixes, suffixes, lengths, reversible encodings, hashes or fingerprints. A separately approved legacy compatibility renderer may use only a fixed `[REDACTED]` token independent of the original, never as a metric dimension. No such field is added to v1. Debug level, sampling, faults, retries and fallback sinks do not relax this rule.
4. **No tenant identifiers.** Do not emit tenant IDs, aliases, names, stable hashes or pseudonyms, including in baggage, resource attributes and exemplars. Only approved aggregate budget observations are allowed, never per-tenant values. Context is not authority, a route or rate key, or an idempotency key. Protected handlers still require fresh identity/membership/resource/policy checks. Separately authorized business envelopes and [required domain audit/trails](../adr/ADR-0039-configurable-audit-trails.md) retain their own projection, access and failure semantics; never mirror them to telemetry. Best-effort telemetry cannot replace required audit persistence.
5. **Keep observation separate from application results.** Preserve the synchronous observer and owned cleanup boundaries; observers must return promptly and must not wait for dispatch, drain or collectors. A future exporter buffers within finite limits and drops on overflow rather than blocking callers or doing inline network I/O. Telemetry failure must not change application results. Treat exported trace/span IDs as restricted diagnostic data; no export until access, retention and deletion are approved. Publish only synthetic inputs and aggregate comparisons, not private fixtures or arbitrary raw captures.

Synthetic example: `event_name=cehpalon.operation.dispatch.completed`, `timestamp_utc=2000-01-01T00:00:00Z`, `severity=info`, `outcome=success`, `duration_ms=2`, `trace_id=01010101010101010101010101010101`, `span_id=0202020202020202`. With no eligible context omit both IDs. Adding `tenant_id=synthetic-tenant-a` violates the contract even though it is synthetic.

## Non-goals and review questions

- No implementation of spans, propagation, adapters, exporters, storage, alerting, SLOs or a tenant debug exception. This draft does not integrate deadlines/cancellation/rate or resource budgets, measure overhead, or prove shutdown/recovery under the other #18 acceptance items. Original gates remain unchanged.
- Before implementation: confirm exact native outcomes and unit mappings on the pinned revision; choose host span boundaries and a safe ASP.NET ingress hook; define broker carrier encoding and verified producer provenance at relay/replay boundaries; agree on sampler settings, sink access and retention/deletion rules, drop accounting and actual series limits after bucket expansion. No backend is selected.
- Future evidence: pin revision, SDK, host/profile and providers. Test trusted/untrusted, absent/duplicate/malformed/oversize context, egress interception, sibling and independent requests, async scope reset, Worker replay/duplicate delivery and disabled/throwing/full sinks. Use synthetic sentinels to check buffers and final export for leaks on successful, rejected, cancelled and faulted paths; assert the metric label budget. These are proposed checks, not results. **M0 remains unchanged**.
