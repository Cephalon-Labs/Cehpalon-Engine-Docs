# ADR0015 — Bounded tooling measurement

Date: 2026-10-30 (Asia/Bangkok). Accepted for the user-authorized continuation of existing #20 after scaffold/contract correctness. #3 stays Blocked; full #20 Benchmark remains Planned and maturity M0. This phase records observed tooling costs; it does not invent performance qualification budgets on a shared, non-reference host.

Measure the actual Roslyn generator and actual CLI/generated consumer with the pinned SDK and locked packages. The compiler comparison is generated DriverRun+PEEmit versus PEEmit of an independently authored typed registry prepared outside the clock. Both use equivalent valid handlers/contracts and actually emit assemblies. This measures extra generation/compilation work over already-authored registrations, not human authoring time, IDE latency or a universal generator/framework speedup. Parsing, metadata reference loading and edited/manual source preparation are outside both clocks and explicitly disclosed.

Separate fresh compiler/driver graphs, retained no-op state and retained single-operation-ID edits. Mirror state retention in the manual path; compare canonical registration structure plus real lazy-factory/dispatch/disposal behavior outside timing. Do not require equal PE hashes across paths; record hashes as provenance. Use fixed case/order/primer/repetition counts and track incremental cache behavior separately from timed drivers. Measure monotonic wall ticks, process CPU, total process allocations and GC counts, including raw zero CPU quantization; no forced collection, outlier filtering or selective reruns.

CLI new/validate and selected offline schema export are separate absolute-cost observations with no invented competitive baseline. Each launch is a real fresh process after declared primers, with OS/filesystem caches primed; this is not cold disk or same-process warm execution. Verify every result/artifact/counter after its launch clock. Record enabled GraphQL schema construction honestly, disabled work as zero, and preserve all primer/measured/failed output.

Correctness and measurement completeness have strict100% thresholds. Performance remains Observed; coefficient of variation above15% flags unstable inference, not a qualification pass/fail budget. Full reference/platform/CI qualification and timing budgets require separate evidence and are not cleared by these observations. See the [fixed predeclared protocol](../validation/tooling-benchmark-protocol.md).
