## Implemented behavior

Optional TenantChangeAuditOutboxExecutor<TSession> validates actual catalog/rules before its provider factory. The callback receives a project-owned mutation Session, separated from Stage/Commit/Rollback/disposal lifecycle. The provider must bind that Session and the checked redacted audit record to one actual transaction. Existing direct recording, preparation and Execute APIs remain compatible. Shared catalog/record projection is factored without a fake/no-op sink; runtime types remain in Audit.cs and fixed embedded source inventories17/32 stay unchanged.

Before commit, callback/projection/stage/cancellation/scope failures attempt private-budget rollback; only acknowledged rollback before any commit attempt yields RolledBack. Failed rollback is RollbackUnknown. Commit invocation errors are CommitUnknown, with no automatic retry or rollback claim. Commit acknowledgement yields Committed, including later cancellation/scope/disposal failures that withhold the result. Typed failures retain primary, rollback and disposal errors. Checks after Session access and awaited disposal prevent a stale successful result. Budgets are cooperative commit/rollback I/O budgets, not a total mutation deadline or forced cancellation. Effects outside the trusted Session are not rollback-covered.

Outbox-mode business success means committed domain change plus pending audit record, not delivered history. Explicit relay uses a new checked audit-outbox/tenant/relay service context, independently of the original writer/request/session. Ordinary writer/history permissions do not grant relay authority. Immutable claims contain at most128 unique records for one tenant; whole-batch validation precedes delivery. Local UTC+monotonic checks supplement authoritative provider tenant/lease/receipt/expiry fencing. Acknowledgement follows sink acceptance; release relinquishes only the old fence, even during cleanup after scope/caller failure. Checks after awaited release also withhold stale results.

AppendOnceAsync requires identical-payload deduplication by tenant+receipt ID and rejects conflicts. Sink success followed by ack loss/expiry replays the original frozen/redacted payload. A partial batch can already have earlier acknowledgements. No timer/background activation, record dropping/dead-letter, automatic business retry, cross-call mutation idempotency or backend exactly-once. Current history policy can further hide fields, but cannot restore/redact queued material retroactively without explicit provider migration/retention. Disabled outbox activation invokes no outbox provider/stage/relay work; independently required ordinary business database transactions can still occur.
