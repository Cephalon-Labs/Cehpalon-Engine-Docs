# CoreCLR GC wait probes for workers born after attachment

One WSL 6.18.58.1 fixture attached both verified CoreCLR instruction sites with a single `BPF_TRACE_UPROBE_MULTI` link. The link's kernel receipt identifies the owned TGID, the pinned image and two offset/cookie pairs. The installed release source filters the address space and checks the thread group. The BPF program independently checks the local TGID before publishing a receipt. Both named workers were created after attachment. The link stayed attached through both lifetimes without per-worker callsite probes or a replacement link. [Pinned kernel implementation](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/a989171929bed4ea681be8d77333845ce1c1732a/kernel/trace/bpf_trace.c#L3063-L3115) · [Frozen protocol](../validation/coreclr-gc-wait-newborn.md)

The peer process used the same CoreCLR image and exercised two call/return pairs under independent per-TID controls. The owned link produced zero peer records. The target's two no-GC arms also produced zero callsite records. In the 20-collection arms, the first newborn produced two pairs and the second produced six. Both mapped to fresh scheduler generations 4 and 5. Birth, initial wake, switch, exit and free records were present, and every newborn call and return fell strictly between its own birth and exit. The scheduler channel accounted for 17,494 records across five enrolled generations with identical detached reads.

The initial audit rejected the capture because it assumed all target records would come from the two named workers. The TGID-scoped link also recorded eight complete pairs on two verified pre-existing threads of the same process. The corrected offline audit retained those separately, verified the call/return order by TID and reconciled all 32 records with the original detached ledger. No recapture was needed. Both native channels recorded zero terminal loss or faults and their observed recursion miss counts were zero. Six tests passed. The failed audit report, original source and capture are kept alongside the replay. [Delivery](../evidence/coreclr-gc-wait-newborn/delivery.json)

The test establishes process-local observation of later-created threads on the pinned configuration. It does not establish capacity and coverage for the full Roslyn run, whether the callee blocked, or the amount of GC or scheduler delay. #20 stays In Progress, #3 stays Blocked and the original benchmark gates apply.

A subsequent bounded Roslyn run recorded the exact worker wait callsite and identified six strict nested GC and scheduler observations. [Roslyn callsite result](roslyn-gc-callsite.md)
